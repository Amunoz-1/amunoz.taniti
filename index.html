Welcome to Taniti!

Taniti is a small, tropical island in the Pacific. While the island has an area of less than 500 square miles, the terrain is varied and includes both sandy and rocky beaches, a small but safe harbor, lush tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior that includes a small, active volcano.

Island Highlights & Geography

Varied Tropical Terrain

Explore less than 500 square miles of diverse landscapes, ranging from the white, sandy beaches encircling Yellow Leaf Bay to rocky coastlines, dense tropical rainforests, and a mountainous volcanic interior.

Culture & Heritage

Taniti is home to an indigenous population of about 20,000 residents. Historically anchored in fishing and agriculture, our island now warmly welcomes visitors from around the world to experience our native architecture, fresh cuisine, and natural wonders.

Food & Dining

From freshly caught Pacific seafood to familiar international favorites and convenient self-catering markets, Taniti offers dining and grocery options to suit every palate and schedule.

Restaurants on Taniti

Taniti currently features 10 restaurants across the island, divided into three distinct culinary styles:

Local Fish & Rice Restaurants (5 Locations)

Five of our restaurants specialize in authentic Tanitian cuisine, serving mostly freshly caught local fish paired with traditional rice dishes.

American-Style Dining (3 Locations)

Three restaurants offer classic American-style meals for visitors seeking familiar comfort food during their stay.

Pan-Asian Cuisine (2 Locations)

Two restaurants serve upscale and casual Pan-Asian cuisine, blending flavors from across the Pacific and Asia.

Grocery & Convenience Stores

For visitors staying in bed and breakfasts, hostels, or extended lodging who prefer to prepare their own meals or stock up on travel essentials, Taniti provides five retail food markets:

  • Supermarkets (2 Locations): Full-service supermarkets carrying a wide selection of produce, meats, pantry staples, and household goods.
  • Smaller Grocery Stores (2 Locations): Neighborhood markets ideal for quick daily ingredients, fresh fruit, and local snacks.
  • 24-Hour Convenience Store (1 Location): Open 24 hours a day to provide late-night snacks, beverages, and travel necessities at any hour.

Dining & Alcohol Advisory

  • Alcohol Service Hours: Please note that alcohol is not allowed to be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m.
  • Legal Drinking Age: The drinking age on Taniti is 18, though it is not strictly enforced.
  • Holiday Closures: Taniti observes a large number of national holidays; many restaurants will be closed on these days, so visitors should plan meals accordingly.

Lodging & Accommodations

Taniti has a wide variety of lodging that ranges from an inexpensive hostel to one large, four-star resort. Whether you are a solo backpacker, a couple seeking a romantic getaway, or a family on vacation, you will find safe, comfortable accommodations.

Accommodation Options

Small Family-Owned Hotels

Experience warm Tanitian hospitality at one of our many small, family-owned hotels located near Taniti City, Merriton Landing, and Yellow Leaf Bay.

Bed & Breakfast

Choose from a growing number of charming bed and breakfasts across the island for a cozy, personalized stay.

Budget-Friendly Hostel

Travelers watching their budget can take advantage of our inexpensive hostel, ideal for backpackers and social travelers.

Four-Star Luxury Resort

Indulge in upscale beachfront accommodations, world-class dining, relaxing spa amenities, and curated island excursions at Taniti’s premier resort.

Government Quality & Safety Standards

Your peace of mind is guaranteed: all types of lodging on Taniti are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government to ensure high standards of cleanliness, safety, and hospitality.

Popular Areas to Stay

  • Taniti City: Features native architecture, flat walkable streets, public bus access, and direct proximity to the white sandy beaches of Yellow Leaf Bay.
  • Merriton Landing: A rapidly developing area on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay that many tourists choose as their home base because it is easy to explore on foot and sits close to dining and entertainment.

Activities, Entertainment & Sightseeing

Most people visit Taniti to enjoy the beaches, explore the rainforest, and visit the volcano. Beyond our natural wonders, Taniti offers a vibrant mix of guided excursions, water sports, cultural venues, and nightlife—much of it centered in Merriton Landing on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay.

Sightseeing & Island Tours

Taniti City & Yellow Leaf Bay

Most tourists spend much of their time in Taniti City, admiring its native architecture and relaxing on the nearby white, sandy beaches that encircle Yellow Leaf Bay.

Volcano & Rainforest Excursions

Embark on scenic hikes through lush tropical rainforests or take guided visits up into the mountainous interior to see Taniti’s small, active volcano.

Guided Island Tours

See the entirety of the island with ease by booking organized boat tours around the coastline or bus tours through the interior.

Outdoor & Water Adventures

  • Snorkeling: Discover vibrant Pacific marine life in our clear coastal waters.
  • Chartered Fishing Tours: Head out from our safe harbor with experienced local captains.
  • Rainforest Zip-Lining: Soar through the lush tropical canopy on thrilling zip-line courses.
  • Helicopter Rides: Take in breathtaking aerial views of the volcano, rainforests, and coastline.
  • Nine-Hole Golf Course (Coming Next Year): A brand-new nine-hole golf course is currently in development and is scheduled to be operational by next year.

Culture, Indoor Fun & Nightlife (Merriton Landing)

Many of these entertainment venues are located in Merriton Landing, a rapidly developing, walkable district on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay:

  • Museums & Art: Visit the local history museum and browse local art galleries.
  • Pubs & Microbrewery: Relax at several local pubs, including a craft microbrewery.
  • Nightlife & Dancing: Dance the night away at Taniti’s new dance club.
  • Family & Indoor Entertainment: Catch a film at the movie theater, play games at the arcade, or enjoy a round of bowling.

Transportation

Getting to Taniti and traveling around our island is simple and convenient, whether you prefer flying, arriving by cruise ship, riding public transit, renting a vehicle, or exploring on foot.

Arriving on Taniti

By Air (Taniti Airport)

Almost all visitors arrive on Taniti by air. Taniti is served by a small airport that currently accommodates small jets and propeller planes. We are actively expanding the airport so that larger commercial jets will be able to land on the island within the next few years.

By Sea (Yellow Leaf Bay Cruise Dock)

Some visitors arrive aboard a small cruise ship that docks safely in Yellow Leaf Bay for one night per week.

Ground Transportation & Getting Around

Public & Private Buses

Public buses serve Taniti City and run daily from 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m. To explore destinations outside the city, private buses serve the rest of the island.

Taxis & Rental Cars

Taxis are readily available throughout Taniti City. Visitors who prefer to drive themselves can rent a car from a local rental agency located conveniently near the airport.

Bicycle Rentals

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors across the island. Please note: helmets are strictly required by law on Taniti.

Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. In addition, the rapidly developing area surrounding Merriton Landing on the north side of Yellow Leaf Bay is compact and easy to explore on foot.

About Taniti & Frequently Requested Information

Learn more about our island's history, people, and economy, and review our essential travel facts to ensure a smooth and enjoyable trip.

Our Island, People & Economy

Taniti is a small tropical island in the Pacific covering an area of less than 500 square miles. Its landscape spans sandy and rocky beaches, a safe harbor, dense tropical rainforests, and a mountainous interior featuring a small active volcano.

The island is home to an indigenous population of approximately 20,000 people. Until a recent increase in tourism, most of the Tanitian economy was dominated by traditional fishing and agriculture.

Frequently Requested Visitor Information (FAQ)

Currency & Payments

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar (USD) as its official currency, though many businesses also accept euros and yen. Several local banks facilitate currency exchange, and most businesses accept major credit cards.

Power Outlets & Electricity

Power outlets on Taniti are 120 volts—the exact same standard used in the United States.

Language & Communication

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. However, very little English is spoken in rural areas, particularly among older residents.

Healthcare & Medical Services

Taniti is served by one main hospital—which employs many multilingual staff members—as well as several medical clinics.

Safety & Crime

Violent crime is very rare on Taniti. However, as tourism has increased, there have been more reports of pickpocketing and other petty crimes, so visitors are encouraged to keep valuables secure.

Alcohol Laws

Alcohol may not be served or sold between the hours of midnight and 9:00 a.m. The legal drinking age is 18, though it is not strictly enforced.

National Holidays

Taniti celebrates a large number of national holidays. Because many tourist attractions and restaurants close on holidays, visitors should check the calendar and plan their itineraries accordingly.
